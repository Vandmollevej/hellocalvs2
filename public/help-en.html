Getting started

What is Hello Cal?

Hello Cal helps you keep track of what you eat and drink, so you can follow calories and nutrients day by day – along with things like weight, water, sleep and body measurements.

How do I change my goals?

Under Goals you can see your goals, create a new one and view upcoming goals.

Go to Goals
Can I see the introduction again?

Yes. Tap Start the guide at the top of the Help center, or go to Settings and tap Learn the app.

Start the guide

Logging food

How do I add food?

Tap the plus button on the front page. Here you can search for a food, scan with the camera, speak using the microphone or pick one of your own dishes.

Search for a food
How do I scan a barcode?
  1. Tap the plus button and choose the camera.
  2. Hold the barcode inside the frame until it has been read.
  3. Check the amount and save.

If scanning is being difficult, make sure there is good light and hold the phone steady.

Open the camera
How do I log with my voice?

Tap the plus button and choose the microphone. Say what you have eaten, and the app finds the foods.

Open the microphone
The item doesn't exist – what do I do?

You can create the item yourself by taking photos of the packaging and the nutrition label.

Create an item
How do I delete an entry?

Swipe on the entry in today's list. Here you can delete it, save the item as a favourite or report a mistake in the item's data.

Can I move an entry to a different time?

Yes. In the Calendar you can drag the entry to the right time.

Go to Calendar
Does the app work without internet?

If you create an item without a connection, the app saves it and sends it automatically as soon as you are online again.

How do I delete my account?

You do it yourself: go to Profile → Profile and find "Close account" at the bottom of the page. You are logged out and any subscription is cancelled. If you change your mind, log in again within 3 months — then the account is open again. After 3 months your personal data is deleted.

If you want your data deleted immediately, use the "Delete my data" button in the same place. This cannot be undone. In the privacy policy you can read what happens to your data.

Go to Profile
